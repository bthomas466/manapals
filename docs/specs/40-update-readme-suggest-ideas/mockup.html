ManaPals

Your Magic collection, shared with your playgroup. Import your cards, see your friends' binders, and find trades both of you will like.

▶ Try it: manapals.vercel.app

ManaPals is not affiliated with Manabox or SkillDevs SC.

What it is

ManaPals is a companion app for Magic: The Gathering players. Import your Manabox export, then share your collection with friends and see what you could trade.

Features

Available today

  • Sign in with Google
  • Import your Manabox CSV, with card data from Scryfall
  • Guided setup for new users
  • Browse your collection: search, filter, sort (1,000+ cards fine)
  • Collection summary: total cards and estimated value
  • Mark cards and binders as available to trade
  • Wishlist of cards you want
  • Trade Match with a friend
  • Friend requests and profile pages at /u/username
  • Friends-only collections by default; opt in to public
  • Works on phones, tablets and desktops

Not yet

  • Apple sign-in
  • Notifications
  • Link previews
  • "Last synced" on profiles
  • Shareable card links
  • Public discovery, other CSV formats, analytics

Suggest an idea or report a problem

  2. The maintainer (@bthomas466) reviews it.
  3. If it's a good fit, it may get built, and the issue is updated.

Not every suggestion will be built, and there is no promised timeline.
